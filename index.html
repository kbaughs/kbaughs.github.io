HPE  ·  Business Process Analyst  ·  2017 to 2019

Ending repeat Severity 1 SLA breaches

Problem

Critical outages for a Fortune 500 client were consistently running past a 6-hour SLA, sometimes into the next day, because parts and technicians were dispatched separately and rarely arrived together.

Approach

Spotted the coordination gap, then designed a cross-functional outage bridge with a weekly on-call rotation, wrote the hourly update and RCA templates, and ran mock calls so the team was ready before a real outage.

6 weeks

until SLA misses stopped. Rolled out to every enterprise account.

Linden Lab  ·  Customer Success  ·  2024 to present

Cutting manual follow-up from about 40 cases a day to 10

Problem

After a business expansion, daily requests jumped from 30+ to 80+. About half were time-sensitive cases that each needed manual follow-up, and overdue cases sat unresolved for more than a week.

Approach

Designed a process that replaced one-by-one follow-up with a longer response window and a single automated notice, sent each day from a system report using notice templates I wrote. Every day's list is logged in the team's Discord as our record. Rolled the change out to 2,000+ customers.

40 → 10

manual cases a day. Now used on 2 of our 3 accounts, and customers have thanked us for the extra time.

Process design  ·  Reporting  ·  Policy and notice writing

Linden Lab  ·  Internal tool

Discord Reminders: a shared reminder system for the team

Problem

The team had no shared way to set reminders, so recurring tasks kept falling through the cracks.

Approach

Designed a Discord bot that reads a config notecard in Second Life and sorts entries into daily, weekly, monthly and date-specific reminders. Set up daily checks on our action queue and trackers, weekly reporting and finance tasks, monthly competitor pricing reviews, and one-off dates like events, deadlines and maintenance windows. I maintain and update the code weekly.

4 cadences

daily, weekly, monthly and date-specific reminders, all managed from one config notecard.

LSL  ·  Python  ·  JavaScript  ·  Discord webhooks

Linden Lab  ·  Customer Success  ·  2026

Making the case for a social media presence

Problem

Competitors had active social media and we had none, so we were missing a channel for customer outreach and engagement.

Approach

Raised the gap with leadership and built the case until I got approval. Set up three social platforms, each with its own campaign, a weekly posting schedule and a content plan: customer spotlights, a business feature program, rotating promos, referral bonuses and monthly giveaways. Created brand mockups, set tracking hashtags and trained the team to post.

Nov 5

launch, with the content plan, brand mockups and team training already in place.
