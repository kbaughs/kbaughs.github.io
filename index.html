About me

The problem behind the problem.

I started on IT helpdesks, moved into partner support and team leadership at HPE, and spent two years there as the only Business Process Analyst on my team, working alongside a BPA team in Bangalore. Since then I have worked in customer success at Linden Lab and built my own online store on the side.

What I do

How I help teams work better

Requirements gathering

I turn requests like "we need a dashboard" into specific business and functional requirements before anyone builds anything.

Process mapping

I document how work really happens today, map the future state in Visio, and mark where handoffs and delays pile up.

Root cause analysis

I dig past the first explanation, using ticket data, interviews and observation to find why a problem keeps coming back.

Data analysis

I clean and explore data with Python to answer a specific business question, then explain what the results mean in plain language.

Stakeholder alignment

I get support, operations and leadership looking at the same picture of the problem, so a fix has buy-in before it rolls out.

Documentation

I write SOPs and build SharePoint sites so an improved process keeps working after the project ends.

Do critics and players agree?

Problem

Critic scores and player scores often tell different stories about the same game. I wanted to know which factors line up with success for each group, and where they split.

Approach

The dataset had no revenue, so I reframed "success" around Metacritic and user scores. I cleaned the data, then compared results by genre, platform, developer, multiplayer support and release year.
